# Page-by-page build-out guide

A single ordered track through every page in the app. Each step is self-contained: finish it, verify it, then move to the next. No step depends on a later one.

## Protocol direction to carry through the build

The product model is a Cardano RealFi protocol built around two clearly separated tokens:

- **USDR:** a dollar-referenced stablecoin intended for 1:1 mint and redemption by whitelisted institutional market makers. Holding USDR alone does not generate yield.
- **sUSDR:** the staked form of USDR. It represents participation in the underlying portfolio and accrues yield generated by that portfolio.

All yield claims and portfolio figures must be grounded in the underlying real-world-asset portfolio, which may include liquid assets such as T-bills and money market funds, alongside higher-yield and less-liquid exposure such as direct fintech lending and private credit. The UI must distinguish verified data, pending attestations, and unavailable data rather than inventing values.

### Cross-cutting protocol requirements

1. **Peg stability:** Model institutional mint/redemption, whitelisting, liquidity coverage, and the role of arbitrage in correcting deviations from the $1 reference. Do not imply that a peg is guaranteed.
2. **Redemption liquidity:** Track the liquid portfolio allocation designated for redemptions and compare it with outstanding USDR redemption demand. Surface a clear warning when the data is stale, incomplete, or insufficient; never claim that all demands can be met without verified data.
3. **Stress testing:** Add a transparent risk view for redemption scenarios, liquidity shocks, leverage exposure, and loss assumptions. Show methodology, inputs, timestamp, and status for each result.
4. **Leverage controls:** Treat looping as an opt-in, governance-controlled strategy with explicit collateral, liquidation, utilization, and downside-risk metrics. No leverage action should be available without the required policy and authorization checks.
5. **Transparency and valuation:** Build around independent valuation, fund administration, and weekly attestations. Every NAV, share-price, reserve, and impact figure needs a source, valuation date, attestation status, and explorer/database reference where applicable.
6. **SPO utility:** Represent Stake Pool Operators as verified distribution and education partners, using proof of real human on-chain activity where available. SPO activity must be anti-bot reviewed and clearly separated from financial performance data.
7. **Multi-chain strategy:** Treat other networks, including Ethereum, as liquidity access and settlement integrations while keeping the canonical asset, accounting, governance, and value-flow records tied back to Cardano. Add explicit bridge and cross-chain risk states before exposing multi-chain actions.
8. **Market fit and eligibility:** Prioritize stablecoin and RWA-tokenization workflows for markets with strong dollar-exposure demand, while enforcing the protocol's jurisdictional eligibility framework. Do not bypass eligibility checks or imply availability where it has not been confirmed.

## Order and rationale

Public/marketing pages first (they are the entry point and the only pages a stranger sees), then the authenticated core, then operator/admin surfaces, then diagnostics. Protocol controls and honest data states are implemented before any growth or presentation layer depends on them.

### Step 1 — `/` Landing (`index.tsx`)

Remove the `ASSETS` mock import. Feature real rows from the `assets` table (server-loaded, cached) with an honest empty state when none are published. Introduce the USDR/sUSDR distinction, RWA-backed yield explanation, jurisdictional eligibility language, and transparency/attestation status without promising returns or peg certainty. Keep the hero, tighten the single H1, and confirm title/description/og tags.

### Step 2 — `/marketplace` list (`marketplace.tsx`)

Already live-queried. Polish search + category filter behaviour against real rows, skeleton loading state, empty state copy, and per-card links to the detail route. Add filters or labels for asset liquidity profile, RWA category, eligibility status, and attestation freshness only when backed by database fields.

### Step 3 — `/marketplace/$id` detail (`marketplace.$id.tsx`)

Already live. Add the vault panel: derived vault address, current share price and TVL for that asset from the yield chain reader, plus deposit entry point. Show whether the asset is eligible, independently valued, administered, and recently attested. Add clear separation between USDR holdings and sUSDR positions, and not-found handling for unknown ids with `noindex` metadata.

### Step 4 — `/app` Portfolio (`app.tsx`)

Remove `mock-data` entirely. Positions, ADA/USD totals, and the sparkline series all come from `vault_positions` + `transactions` + on-chain holdings. Distinguish non-yield USDR from yield-bearing sUSDR, show source and freshness for valuation, and provide a zero-state for a wallet with no positions rather than fabricated figures.

### Step 5 — `/yield` Yield engine (`yield.tsx`)

Already reading chain state. Add per-asset selection so the page can show any bootstrapped vault, not just the default, and surface accrual history with tx links to a Cardano explorer. Encode that sUSDR yield comes from verified portfolio accruals, while USDR earns no yield. Add pending-attestation and stale-oracle states; do not display projected APY as realized performance.

### Step 6 — `/governance` + `/governance/new`

List and detail are already live. Finish the loop: proposal detail view, vote counts, and connecting an executed proposal to the vault action it authorizes. Add governance controls for whitelist policy, liquidity reserves, leverage limits, stress-test assumptions, RWA eligibility, attestations, and any cross-chain integration. Submission wizard gets validation and a post-submit confirmation state.

### Step 7 — `/security` (`security.tsx`)

Live sessions, audit log, and the four toggles already persist. Add the wallet-linking step (register a wallet against the signed-in account) since transactions require it, and make each toggle's effect explicit in the UI. Include transaction authorization, withdrawal/redemption controls, whitelisted market-maker checks, and a complete audit trail for privileged protocol actions.

### Step 8 — `/stewardship` (`stewardship.tsx`)

Currently the thinnest page and still on `sparkline` from mock-data. Decide its real source: impact metrics per asset from `assets`/`oracle_feeds`, steward referral counts from the referral store, and verified SPO/distribution activity from recorded chain data. Replace the hardcoded `STL-STEWARD-9F2A` code with the signed-in user's real code. Keep education and human-activity metrics separate from yield and NAV.

### Step 9 — `/invite` (`invite.tsx`)

Replace the mock leaderboard with real aggregated referral confirmations, or remove the leaderboard if the data cannot be sourced honestly. Audit log and anti-abuse UI stay as-is. Add referral attribution safeguards for repeated devices, suspicious farming, and jurisdictionally restricted onboarding where applicable.

### Step 10 — `/operators` (`operators.tsx`)

Admin surface, already gated by master wallet. Remaining: vault list view for every bootstrapped asset with its state UTxO, accrual history per vault alongside the existing build/sign/submit card, and operational controls for reserves, attestations, market-maker allowlists, and stress-test runs. Every privileged action must require the correct role, wallet, threshold signatures, and an immutable audit record.

### Step 11 — `/developers` (`developers.tsx`)

API key management is in place. Add endpoint documentation that matches the actual `/api/public/*` routes, plus example requests. Document data provenance, attestation timestamps, chain/network scope, USDR versus sUSDR semantics, and explicit limitations for any cross-chain data.

### Step 12 — Diagnostics: `/testnet`, `/blockfrost-health`, `/upgrade/*`

Lowest priority. Confirm each still works after the above changes, mark diagnostic pages `noindex`, and verify the upgrade return flow. Add checks for oracle freshness, attestation freshness, reserve/liquidity coverage, network mismatch, and contract-version compatibility.

### Step 13 — Cleanup and production-readiness review

Delete `src/lib/mock-data.ts` once no page imports it. Re-check every route's `head()` for unique title/description/og tags. Verify that no page fabricates NAV, APY, reserves, peg status, impact, liquidity, or user activity. Review smart-contract calls for mint/redeem, USDR-to-sUSDR conversion, withdrawal, accrual, whitelist, oracle/attestation, governance, and cross-chain authorization paths before mainnet readiness is claimed.

## Technical notes

- Data reads follow the existing pattern: server function or `queryOptions` + `ensureQueryData` in the loader, `useSuspenseQuery` in the component. No `useEffect` fetching.
- Any page reading authenticated data keeps its loader out of public routes; protected reads go through the component with `useServerFn`.
- Chain reads keep using the existing `plutus-cbor` / `yield-chain.functions.ts` path so the Blockfrost key stays server-side.
- Every financial or operational metric must carry source, timestamp, and status; missing or unverified values render an empty or pending state.
- No fabricated figures at any step, and no marketing copy should imply guaranteed yield, guaranteed liquidity, guaranteed peg stability, or regulatory availability.
